18AI / RESEARCH & INSIGHTS

Understanding what's next.
Building what matters now.

Clear perspectives on foundational AI, emerging agent architectures and long-term research — separating established methods from open research questions.

FIELD NOTE 01 / FOUNDATIONS

Generative AI

Models that generate text, code, images, audio and other media. Learn how prompts, grounding, RAG, fine-tuning and evaluation affect reliability.

Business lens: knowledge support, content, productivity, coding assistance.
FIELD NOTE 02 / SYSTEMS

Agentic AI

Systems that plan and use tools across tasks. Explore agent loops, orchestration, MCP, permission boundaries, human approval and rollback.

Business lens: workflow execution with controlled autonomy.
FIELD NOTE 03 / INFRASTRUCTURE

RAG & Context Engineering

Connect models to trusted knowledge through retrieval, embeddings, graph relationships and structured context. Focus on provenance, relevance and freshness.

Business lens: domain-grounded decisions and evidence.
FIELD NOTE 04 / FRONTIER

AGI & General Intelligence

Artificial general intelligence is a debated, research-level concept. Definitions and benchmarks vary; do not confuse advanced assistants with established AGI.

Business lens: scenario planning, not unproven capability claims.
FIELD NOTE 05 / MULTIMODAL

Multimodal AI

Systems combining language, images, audio and video, enabling richer interfaces and new educational and operational workflows.

Business lens: visual learning, document understanding, accessibility.
FIELD NOTE 06 / ASSURANCE

Responsible & Evaluated AI

Security, privacy, traceability, human-in-the-loop and continuous testing turn a compelling prototype into a safer, dependable service.

Business lens: measurable quality, risk management and trust.
